## Caption

How do you defend yourself against something you never did?

Someone hunting for a flaw will find one, even where none exists. The accusation shows what they were searching for, not what you did.

ਜਉ ਦੇਖੈ ਛਿਦ੍ਰੁ ਤਉ ਨਿੰਦਕੁ ਉਮਾਹੈ ਭਲੋ ਦੇਖਿ ਦੁਖ ਭਰੀਐ ॥
When he sees a flaw, the slanderer is delighted; seeing good, he is filled with pain.

ਨਿੰਦਕੁ ਐਸੇ ਹੀ ਝਰਿ ਪਰੀਐ ॥ ਇਹ ਨੀਸਾਨੀ ਸੁਨਹੁ ਤੁਮ ਭਾਈ ਜਿਉ ਕਾਲਰ ਭੀਤਿ ਗਿਰੀਐ ॥
Thus the slanderer crumbles away — like a wall of saltpetre.

ਨਾਨਕ ਕਾ ਰਾਖਾ ਆਪਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਕਿਆ ਮਾਨਸ ਬਪੁਰੇ ਕਰੀਐ ॥
God Himself is Nanak’s protector; what can wretched mortals do?

ਅਨਬੋਲੇ ਕਉ ਤੁਹੀ ਪਛਾਨਹਿ ਜੋ ਜੀਅਨ ਮਹਿ ਹੋਤਾ ॥
Without a word spoken, You know whatever is within every heart.

— Guru Arjan Dev Ji, Raag Bilaval, Ang 823

Let the wall fall on its own. Keep your conduct clean. Waheguru is already your witness.
