# MedRise Gurbani — Sikhi dates content calendar (Sept 2026 → Apr 2027)

Dates follow the SGPC-observed Nanakshahi 558 calendar (cross-checked on two sources). Where the Bikrami and the original Nanakshahi differ (e.g., Shaheedi Guru Tegh Bahadur), the SGPC date is used, since that is what gurdwaras and sangat observe. Verify against your local gurdwara for the shaheedi week in December.

Each entry: date · observance · **post angle in the series voice** · Gurbani anchor · MedRise release that fits (for the CTA).

## How this plugs into the posting workflow
1. Before building any carousel, check this file: if a major date falls within 2 days, that date takes the noon slot and the planned post moves.
2. Major dates get the noon slot; medium dates get the 8 PM carousel; minor dates get a single-slide morning post or are skipped.
3. Shaheedi week (Dec 21–28) is a connected series — no unrelated posts in that window.
4. Time new releases to the matching date: Jo Prabh Ko Milbo Chahe → Nov 11; Ajai Alai → Jan 15; anything on Guru Nanak → Nov 24.
