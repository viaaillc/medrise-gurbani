## Caption

Who called you a failure, and when did you start saying it too?

A label only sticks when you start repeating it. They said it once. You’ve been saying it every day since.

Even Guru Nanak Dev Ji was called names:
ਕੋਈ ਆਖੈ ਭੂਤਨਾ ਕੋ ਕਹੈ ਬੇਤਾਲਾ ॥ ਕੋਈ ਆਖੈ ਆਦਮੀ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਵੇਚਾਰਾ ॥
Some call him a ghost, some a demon; some call him a mere, pitiful man.
— Guru Nanak Dev Ji, Raag Maru, Ang 991

Bhagat Kabir Ji didn’t flinch:
ਨਿੰਦਉ ਨਿੰਦਉ ਮੋ ਕਉ ਲੋਗੁ ਨਿੰਦਉ ॥ ਨਿੰਦਾ ਜਨ ਕਉ ਖਰੀ ਪਿਆਰੀ ॥
Slander me, slander me, go ahead, people, slander me. Slander is dear to the Lord’s servant.
ਨਿੰਦਕੁ ਡੂਬਾ ਹਮ ਉਤਰੇ ਪਾਰਿ ॥
The slanderer drowned. We crossed over.
— Bhagat Kabir Ji, Raag Gauri, Ang 339

They named you. They don’t get to define you.

ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ ਜੀ ਕਾ ਖ਼ਾਲਸਾ, ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ ਜੀ ਕੀ ਫ਼ਤਹਿ 🙏
