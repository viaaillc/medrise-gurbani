## Caption

Have you ever trusted someone close with your secret, only to hear it come back as gossip about you?

That hurt runs deep, because the betrayal came from someone who knew you.

ਨਿੰਦਾ ਭਲੀ ਕਿਸੈ ਕੀ ਨਾਹੀ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਮੁਗਧ ਕਰੰਨਿ ॥
Slandering anyone is not good; only the foolish and self-willed do it.
— Guru Amar Das Ji, Raag Suhi, Ang 755

The slander says more about them than about you. What you shared in trust was never the mistake. Breaking that trust was theirs.

Keep your heart clean. Leave their actions to Waheguru.
