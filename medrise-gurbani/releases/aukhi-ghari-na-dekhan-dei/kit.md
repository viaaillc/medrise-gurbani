Aukhee gharee na dhekhan dheiee apanaa biradh samaale.
He does not let His own see a hard hour; He keeps to His age-old nature of caring for them.

ਹਾਥ ਦੇਇ ਰਾਖੈ ਅਪਨੇ ਕਉ ਸਾਸਿ ਸਾਸਿ ਪ੍ਰਤਿਪਾਲੇ ॥੧॥
Haath dhei raakhai apane kau saas saas pratipaale. ||1||
Giving His own hand, He shields His own and sustains them with every breath. ||1||

ਪ੍ਰਭ ਸਿਉ ਲਾਗਿ ਰਹਿਓ ਮੇਰਾ ਚੀਤੁ ॥
Prabh siau laag rahio meraa cheet.
My mind stays attached to God.

ਆਦਿ ਅੰਤਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸਦਾ ਸਹਾਈ ਧੰਨੁ ਹਮਾਰਾ ਮੀਤੁ ॥ ਰਹਾਉ ॥
Aadh ant prabh sadhaa sahaaee dhann hamaaraa meet. || Rahaau ||
From beginning to end, God is always my help; blessed is my Friend. || Pause ||

ਮਨਿ ਬਿਲਾਸ ਭਏ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਕੇ ਅਚਰਜ ਦੇਖਿ ਬਡਾਈ ॥
Man bilaas bhe saahib ke acharaj dhekh baddaaee.
Seeing the Master's wondrous greatness, my mind is filled with joy.

ਹਰਿ ਸਿਮਰਿ ਸਿਮਰਿ ਆਨਦ ਕਰਿ ਨਾਨਕ ਪ੍ਰਭਿ ਪੂਰਨ ਪੈਜ ਰਖਾਈ ॥੨॥੧੫॥੪੬॥
Har simar simar aanadh kar Naanak prabh pooran paij rakhaaee. ||2||15||46||
Remember the Lord again and again and live in bliss, O Nanak; God has kept His servant's honor in full. ||2||15||46||
